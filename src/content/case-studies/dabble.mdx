Dabble is a web app I designed and built to archive and visualize the
moments of my own dance practice, and the other activities around it that
still shape my trajectory. It takes the spiral timeline I co-designed for
my master's thesis, [ChoreoMapper](/work/choreomapper), and turns the
prototype into a working tool, with three linked visualizations, a
flexible data model for imperfect memories, and space to reflect on past
moments in hindsight.

> What value comes out of visualizing the moments of a practice that
> spans different styles, and activities beyond it?

## Project Context & Motivation

### From ChoreoMapper to Dabble

For my master's thesis, under the supervision of Eva Hornecker and Sarah
Fdili Alaoui, I conducted participatory research with female
choreographers and dance practitioners to co-design ChoreoMapper, an
interactive digital tool for dancers to trace their careers. It used
moments in the dancers' lives, both dance and non-dance related, as data
points and visualized them in a spiral.

The final artifact of the thesis was a high-fidelity prototype. Dabble is
my continuation of that work: an implementation of the idea as a real web
app that I could use myself and that anyone else can try.

### Personal Motivation

I myself am a dancer, and my practice spans different styles as well as
activities that aren't directly related to dance but still impact my
trajectory. I wanted to explore what I'd get out of visualizing those
moments for my own archive — so the app comes pre-loaded with my own dance
history as its example data.

## Designing for Imperfect Memories

Logging a hobby after the fact rarely fits a neat form. A lot of
Dabble's design work went into a data model that accepts entries as people
actually remember them.

### Flexible Date Precision

Entries don't need an exact date. Something can be logged as vaguely as
"October – November 2021," given a real date range for a multi-day event,
or recorded without a time of day if only the day is remembered.

### Dynamic Categories

Categories aren't fixed in advance. When I pick up a new hobby, I can add
a category for it and it's woven into every visualization right away,
with its own color. Categories can be renamed or recolored later, from a
curated palette, colors already in use, or a custom picker.

### Reflections

Any past entry can get dated, backdatable follow-up notes, each with its
own mood, text, and media, so I can capture how I feel about a moment in
hindsight while keeping it linked to the original. Multiple open entries
sit in small bookmark tabs in the sidebar.

## Three Views of One Practice

The same entries can be explored through three visualizations, all driven
by one shared time-range brush selector, so narrowing to a window of time
carries across every view.

### Constellation

Entries appear as stars clustered by activity, echoing a night sky.
Clicking a star opens its entry; the view can be filtered by category or
scrubbed through time.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Constellation view" />

### Timeline

A linear, chronological view of every entry. Multi-day events render as
capsules, and overlapping periods are automatically laid out on separate
tracks so they stay legible.

<ImagePlaceholder label="Timeline view" />

### Spiral

Time coils outward from a center point — the earliest entry at the core,
the most recent at the outer edge. This view comes directly from the
ChoreoMapper prototype.

## Try It Yourself

### Bring Your Own Data

Dabble runs entirely in the browser. Anyone visiting can reset it to a
blank slate and start their own constellation from their own hobbies and
practices, like a digital journal for tracking progress and moments.
Entries can be exported as a portable JSON file and imported again later
to pick up where you left off.

## Future Work

Dabble is still in progress. Features I'm planning next include:

- **Connections between entries** — visualizing links across events, such
  as shared locations, feelings, or life events
- **Material artifacts** — objects tied to a practice or era rather than a
  single date (a pair of shoes, a song, a video that shaped a style),
  linked to the moments they relate to
- **Multi-category entries** — e.g. a trip that involved both shuffle
  dancing and pole & calisthenics training, possibly shown as a blend of
  each category's color
- **Richer media and locations** — photo and voice-note attachments,
  previews of linked videos, and mappable locations
- **A list/table view** for browsing entries outside the visualizations
- **Accounts and cross-device sync**, replacing JSON import/export as the
  main way to save data
